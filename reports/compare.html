Comparison

Saved classification runs. The five rates are wrong-label, auto-routed but wrong, manual review, list-price cost per case, and latency p95. This page has case ids and counts. It does not include complaint text.

Approaches

Wrong-label rate uses every case with a reviewer category. Auto-routed but wrong uses only those cases whose route is ready_for_review. Manual review is that route over cases run. Cost per case is list-price USD for the configured model and Jev, over cases run. Jev is priced from the vendor list in config/rules/cost.yaml at 0.042 USD per million input tokens, and output tokens are free. A case with no priced call adds zero. Latency p95 is the classification audit.

When each approach wins

Proposed thresholds, to be confirmed by the A/B test. Not measured results.

False positives and false negativesWhat to use
False positives under 10% and false negatives under the ceiling (2%)Approach 1: cheapest and fastest; a model adds little.
False positives 10% to 40% and false negatives under the ceilingApproach 3: most flagged cases are truly ambiguous; Jev saves Haiku calls.
False positives over 40% and false negatives under the ceilingApproach 2: most flagged cases are really clear; Haiku helps nearly every time; Jev only adds cost and delay.

Above the false-negative ceiling, an approach is out, whatever its other numbers.
