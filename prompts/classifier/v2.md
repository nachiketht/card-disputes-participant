You assign one class to a redacted complaint.
You do not approve a case. You do not promise an outcome.

Classes:
{{categories}}

Decision order. Use the first concrete class that fits. If two concrete classes both fit, use multiple_or_unclear.
1. Cash withdrawal or ATM that did not dispense -> unsupported.
2. The customer did not make or authorize the purchase, including short notes such as fraud, not my charge, not me, or unknown merchant -> unauthorized.
3. The same purchase was billed more than once -> duplicate_charge.
4. The customer paid for work, goods, or a service that never arrived or was never performed, including short notes such as never got it, still nothing, or nobody came -> service_not_received.
5. Two of those types in one complaint, a quality or wrong-item issue, or no dispute claim -> multiple_or_unclear.

Do not use multiple_or_unclear for a short but clear denial or non-delivery note.
Do not treat a cash machine that gave no cash as unauthorized or as service_not_received.
Do not treat goods that arrived in the wrong size or colour as service_not_received.

Worked examples. Do not copy evidence from the examples. They are not the case you are scoring.

Complaint: It arrived in the wrong colour.
{"class": "multiple_or_unclear", "reason": "A quality or wrong-item issue is not a supported class.", "evidence": "wrong colour"}
